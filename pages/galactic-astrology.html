About the talk

Julia Balaz spent 7 years facilitating QHHT (Quantum Healing Hypnosis Technique, Dolores Cannon's method) past-life regressions for roughly 2,000 people. Over time she noticed a pattern: clients weren't just remembering earthly lives — many regressed to lives in other star systems (different-coloured skies, multiple moons, different numbers of fingers). When she cross-referenced these regressions with their astrological birth charts, she found strong correlations: the star systems people remembered matched fixed-star conjunctions in their natal charts.

She developed this into a modality called galactic astrology / quantum soul guidance, and built a free software tool (galacticastrochart.com) that overlays fixed stars and ancient-Earth asteroids onto a standard natal chart.

"I believe at this point that the birth chart works like an infinite map that is almost a live mechanism that responds to the level of our consciousness and it can reveal to us information that we are ready to receive."

Common message for humanity (from the regressions)

  • Earth was in a kind of quarantine / disconnect from the rest of the cosmos.
  • After the 1945 atomic bomb, Earth sent a call out into the universe for souls to come and anchor more light and love.
  • Millions of souls answered and incarnated here to help Earth through her ascension/awakening.
  • These souls are "code keepers" / "rememberers" — able to perceive multi-dimensionally — through whom Earth is reaching back out to her cosmic family.
  • Every human body on Earth has a genetic link to beings from the stars (many races visited and seeded Earth).
  • The future on Earth (even ~100 years out) was repeatedly seen in regressions as globally peaceful.

How to work out your astrology (per this video)

Julia's method: treat the natal chart as a "live map" that reveals soul origins and unresolved karma. She reads it in a specific order and uses both fixed stars and asteroids.

  1. Get your natal chart. You need birth date, exact birth time, and birth location (city). Free at astro.com (Astrodienst) → "Free Horoscopes → Extended Chart Selection".
  2. Start from the outer planets (Pluto, Neptune, Uranus). These are generational and represent the most ancient, distant soul memories. Look for conjunctions/oppositions/squares to fixed stars (especially Lyra, Orion, Pleiades, Arcturus/Spica, Sirius, Beta Centauri). Hard aspects to Lyra (e.g. square) often indicate the ancient Lyran refugee/evacuation story.
  3. Move inward to personal planets and angles. Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Ascendant, Midheaven. Galactic connections here feel "more recent" and more fully expressed in this life. E.g. Pleiades on the Ascendant, Mercury conjunct Pleiades.
  4. Check the lunar nodes. South Node = experiences mastered (or over-focused on) in past incarnations; North Node = where you're heading this life to balance. The south node sign often correlates with remembered past lives.
  5. Find fixed-star conjunctions. Use a tool that lists which fixed stars are conjunct your planets (within ~1–2°). Key stars from the talk: Pleiades (Alcyone, ~29–30° Taurus), Arcturus (~24° Libra), Spica (~24° Virgo), Sirius (~14° Cancer), Regulus (~29° Leo), Altair (~1° Aquarius), Beta Centauri (~21° Scorpio), Orion Nebula (~23° Gemini), Lyra/Vega (~15° Capricorn).
  6. Look at asteroids for Earth-side ancient lives. In Julia's tool, asteroids named Atlantis, Lemuria, Hyperborea, plus Egyptian deities (Hathor, Isis, etc.) and others. When an asteroid is conjunct a planet or activated by a transit on the day you "remembered," that's a confirmation. She also references a spirit/asteroid called Memoria.
  7. Check transits. When a transiting planet crosses one of these fixed stars/asteroids on a day you had a spontaneous memory or download, that's validation.
  8. Identify your "soul ray color." Ask yourself (or in meditation) what the most dominant color of your soul frequency is — blue (protector/upholder of divine plan), purple (teacher/sage), yellow (joyful communicator), red (builder), orange (artist/creative/sensual). This maps to chakras and informs your theme across incarnations.
"Having awareness of all these ancient connections of who you were, how far you've been, means nothing if you are not expressing your light and love in the present moment with people who are next to you physically right now."

Important caveats (from the talk itself)

  • Julia explicitly says galactic astrology is not for everyone — it's for people who are already experiencing spontaneous memories/recalls and becoming "activated."
  • She warns against using star origins as an ego label to feel superior. The purpose is healing and remembering, not hierarchy.
  • She stresses returning to the present moment and your physical body — the ancient awareness means nothing if you don't express love/light with the people next to you now.
  • Earth was described as being in quarantine; the goal is reconnection with the cosmic family, not leaving Earth. "It's the best place to be at this time."
  • She does not want this to be a distraction from everyday life and relationships.
