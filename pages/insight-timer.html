2 Recording Setup

Record Professional Quality

Your Council recording setup (BlackHole + ffmpeg) is perfect. Here's the Insight Timer optimized workflow.

1
Audio Settings
48kHz / 24-bit WAV → Convert to 44.1kHz / 16-bit MP3 (320kbps) for upload. Insight Timer accepts: MP3, M4A, WAV up to 500MB
2
Structure Template
[30s] Intro & settling → [2-3min] Teaching/guidance → [5-15min] Practice/silence → [30s] Gentle closing → [10s] Fade out
3
Background Music
Use your elemental tracks! Keep music -20dB under voice. Enable "Background Music" toggle on upload so users can adjust mix.
4
Noise Cleanup
MacWhisper transcript → Check for artifacts → Use Audacity (free) for noise reduction if needed → Normalize to -1dB
