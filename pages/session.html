Recording

  • Jitsi's own recording — records everyone, server-side. In a full browser tab: ⋮ → Start recording → Dropbox. First time only, it asks you to connect Dropbox. Only moderators can record.
  • The Record button (bottom of the video) — starts a browser-side recording of your own stream. Appears once you're in the room.
  • Local Audio (⌘⌥⌃M) — records your Mac's audio separately via Hammerspoon, so you get a clean track even if the browser one drops.

Whoever opens the room first becomes the moderator and can record. Everyone else just types a name and joins.
