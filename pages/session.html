Recording — Three Ways

Jitsi built-in server-side

Records everyone, mixed on the server. In a full browser tab: ⋮ → Start recording → Dropbox. Dropbox asks you to connect once.

This page's button browser-side

Starts a recording of your own stream in the browser and downloads it when you stop. Works on the embed, so it only captures the first five minutes there.

Local audio checking…

Records your Mac's audio separately, outside the browser, straight from the meeting tab. Cleanest track of the three, and it runs for the whole session.

Your browser is blocking local access

Chrome asks before a website can talk to this Mac. Look for the icon beside the address bar, choose Allow for local network access, then press Retry. Or skip it entirely and press ⌘⌥⌃M — the recording works the same way.

On the public server, whoever opens the room first must sign in with Google to become moderator — guests just see "no moderators have yet arrived" until then. On your own server, anyone who joins first becomes moderator automatically — no sign-in needed. The moderator is the only one who can start a recording. So: open the room, then ⋮ → Start recording → Dropbox (or use the Record button below). Everyone else just types a name and joins.
