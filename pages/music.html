Collaboration Workflow

How we create music together across distances

1. Linda's Vision

Voice memos with hummed melodies, described feelings, referenced tracks, elemental qualities

2. Daniele Sketches

Piano/DAW sketches in Logic Pro. Shares stems via Dropbox. Iterates based on feedback.

3. Daniele Arranges

Layers the sketch with instrumentation, texture & atmosphere. Refines against Linda's feedback.

4. Finalize & Deliver

Master for session use. Export stems. Upload to course platform. Archive in Dropbox.
